The two numbers are not the same number

71% of farms in the direct sourcing programme are polygon mapped. That is the figure in the sustainability report and in every sales conversation. But only 37% of the volume actually received arrives through a chain of custody that carries that mapping with it. Mapping a farm and mapping the beans you receive are different achievements, and the difference is roughly 70,000 tonnes of EU-bound volume with no defensible file behind it. Sixteen weeks before the regulation applies, this is the number that matters.

Three deadlines, one quarter, four owners

Each of these is currently held by a different function and treated as a separate project. They are the same problem seen from three angles: what the company can prove about where its cocoa came from.

The strategic case, before the compliance case

Why this is a commercial asset

Buyers are already paying for verified deforestation-free material, and beans without traceability documentation have been turned away at origin ports. Traceability has stopped being a reporting attribute and become a price and access attribute.

A processor that can hand a customer a complete, assured file — origin, plot, cut-off date, chain of custody model, and the exact wording they may print — is selling something its competitors cannot. The company that can only hand over a certificate is selling a commodity.

That is the proposition. The compliance deadline is what makes it urgent, not what makes it valuable.

Why it has to survive an auditor

The customer's assurance provider will test the claim, not the intention. Three questions decide it: which chain of custody model carries this volume, what evidence establishes the cut-off date, and has this benefit been claimed by anyone else.

Every module here is built to answer those three in a form that can be handed over, which is why the register carries owners and expiry dates and the balance is reconciled per origin rather than in aggregate.

A proposition that cannot be assured is a proposition with a shelf life of one audit cycle.

Certification is not the answer to this

Certification schemes state plainly that cocoa sourced through segregated or mass balance chains is not traceable to farm level, and that alignment therefore has to be established outside the certification system. A company that has answered the traceability question with a certificate has not answered it. The 90,300 tonnes on the mass balance rung need a separate route to plot data, or they need to stop being sold into the EU as a traceable product.

A group surplus of 600 tonnes conceals origin breaches of 7,900

Netted across all origins the book looks healthy, and that is exactly how it has been reported. Under origin matching it is not one book but six, and two of them are overdrawn — Ghana by 5,800 tonnes and the residual origin group by 2,100. Surplus in Côte d'Ivoire cannot settle a Ghanaian claim. The aggregate view is not a simplification of the truth here; it is the thing preventing anyone from seeing it.

What traceability is worth in carbon

The land use change method is not a technical footnote. It is the single largest number in a cocoa footprint, and the right to use the lower one is earned with plot data.

Read this before the compliance argument

Statistical land use change applies a country-average conversion burden to every tonne from that origin, regardless of the individual plot. Direct land use change assesses the actual plot against the cut-off date. For West African cocoa the gap between the two is not a rounding difference; it is a multiple.

A company without plot data has no basis to use the direct method and must carry the country average. A company with plot data can evidence what happened on the land it actually bought from.

So the traceability investment and the carbon number are the same project. Every tonne moved off the mass balance rung is a tonne that can be assessed directly, and the customer's own FLAG inventory feels the difference immediately. That is the argument that unlocks the budget — not the deadline.

Methodology positions taken, and why they survive challenge

DecisionPosition
Land use change methodDirect assessment only for volume with plot data verified against the 31 December 2020 reference date. Statistical country default for everything else, disclosed separately by volume rather than blended into a single average. A blended figure is the number most often published and the first one an assurance provider unpicks.
Agroforestry removalsQuantified but reported separately from reductions and never netted into a headline figure. Held out of customer-facing claims until permanence, reversal monitoring and traceability conditions under the Land Sector and Removals Standard are contracted, ahead of its effect from 1 January 2027.
Allocation across cocoa productsEconomic allocation between butter, liquor, powder and cake, with the rule published. Butter and powder prices diverge sharply, so allocation choice moves a customer's ingredient footprint materially, and any switch is treated as a baseline recalculation rather than an improvement.
Primary data thresholdEvery footprint issued states its primary data share by percentage of volume. A footprint that does not disclose this cannot be compared with a competitor's, and customers are beginning to ask for it before they ask for the number itself.

    Approval gates for a new claim

    1. Intake and triageNew claim, or an existing register entry? Most requests are the second and should never reach a gate. Triage is what keeps the gates credible.
    2. Chain of custody testWhich rung carries this volume, and does the proposed wording describe that rung or a higher one? This single question kills most bad claims before any methodology work begins.
    3. Evidence and exclusivityCut-off date evidence, plot data status, and confirmation against the origin-matched balance that the benefit has not been sold twice.
    4. Channel and restatementWhich markets, which channels, and precisely what the customer may print. Consumer-facing restatement is approved in writing or refused.
    5. Release and expiryWording locked, owner named, review date set. A claim with no expiry becomes a legacy claim, and legacy claims are what enforcement finds first.

    First ninety days

    Inventory every environmental and social sentence currently in market, in any channel and any language, and test each one against the chain of custody rung that carries the volume. That test alone resolves most of the register and costs nothing but attention.

    Build the origin-matched balance from purchase and sales records and report it once. The first number is always the argument that starts the work.

    Then run the two routes that can land before December — cooperative data plumbing and EU-bound volume reallocation — while opening the trader data negotiation that will not land this cycle and cannot be started later than now.
